# Magiamo (magiamo-dev)

✦ Building Web3 communities of ownership ✦  
From **hospitality workers’ stories** to **global travelers’ journeys**.  

This is my public lab for **OOO Society, Omnikila, and hospiamo.eth** —  
a community like therapy for hospitality & F&B workers, managers, and mentors across the U.S.  

We connect with travelers like friends, bring diverse cultures together,  
and grow **ownership, education, and assets** in what we do every day.  

*“Ownership is love in action.”*

---

### 🌍 What I'm Building
- **OOO Society** → community hub for hospitality workers, travelers, and allies  
- **Omnikila** → story world + mythology for hospitality in the Americas  
- **Hospiamo.eth** → decentralized handle for projects + experiments  
- Broader **Web3 experiments** → nonprofit, social enterprise, and traveler connections  

---

### 🧭 Focus
- Hospitality ownership, trust, and storytelling  
- Web3 onboarding for non-technical communities  
- Community co-creation: songs, stories, archetypes, and experiences  
- Nonprofit & social enterprise models  
- Legacy web/IRL experiments too  

---

### 💠 Why This Matters
Magiamo-dev is **built with and for hundreds of workers, leaders, and mentors** who thrive in their communities.  
I’m helping grow their **ownership, education, and assets** in what they already do so well.  

I’ve spent **3 years studying Web3**, and I’m still only ~10% where I want to be. That’s why I lean on **communities like Common Ground, LUKSO, and UP** — they align naturally with the generosity and love at the core of hospitality. I’ve been welcomed here, and I’m not stopping.
